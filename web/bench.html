Benchmark this model on this PC

Speed (reading prompts of 512 to 32K tokens from scratch and again with them cached, the time to the first word, writing replies of different lengths, writing after a long context, guessing ahead on and off) and a short fixed set of 30 questions from public benchmarks, the same every time: maths, knowledge, code and following instructions. Answers are at temperature 0 with thinking off. It uses the loaded model and its settings, and takes from under a minute (a model all in VRAM) to 10 minutes or more (a big model mostly in RAM). Chats wait while it runs.
